Privacy Policy — Open Claw

WENZL LLC · Effective September 8, 2026 · bandpeace.com

Open Claw is an internal bookkeeping assistant built and used by WENZL LLC. It is a private tool for the company's own books: it has no public sign-up, no third-party users, and no customer accounts.

What data it touches

Open Claw connects to WENZL LLC's own QuickBooks Online company through Intuit's official accounting API, with read and bookkeeping-entry scopes only. It reads the company's own transactions, categories, and reports in order to categorize expenses, stage bills for human approval, and produce financial summaries.

Where data lives

Data retrieved from QuickBooks is processed and stored on WENZL LLC's own computers. Published financial summaries contain category-level totals only — never transactions, counterparty names, or account numbers.

What we never do

  • No selling, renting, or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No advertising or tracking of any person.
  • No collection of data about anyone other than WENZL LLC itself.
  • The tool cannot move money by design — payment approval is always human.
