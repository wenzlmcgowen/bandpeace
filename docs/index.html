How it works

Three steps to open books

  1. 1

    Connect your accounts

    Distributors, PROs, publishing admins, banks, payment apps — each through the provider's own secure login. BandPeace never sees a password.

  2. 2

    We pull the stats

    We pull stats automatically where providers allow it — and take statement uploads where they don't. Royalty statements, per-store streams, gig payments, and expenses reconcile into one honest ledger.

  3. 3

    Your profile goes live

    Once real connections exist, your page goes live and updates itself — dashboard, royalty map, tax cards, downloadable books. The whole money story, no spin.

What you get

P&L dashboard
